- `TPMA`, `PKPK`, `CLPI`, and `PSAT` are the cleanest proxy candidates in this 100-name smoke universe because their accumulation/absorption profile is constructive without extreme 20-day extension.
- `DGWG`, `SUNI`, `PDPP`, `TEBE`, `PACK`, `BLES`, `ALKA`, `DWGL`, and `IATA` show strong positive flow proxies but are already materially extended; the model deliberately reduces their decision score rather than rewarding raw momentum alone.
- `CTBN`, `TCPI`, `RMKO`, `EPAC`, `MDKI`, `CGAS` and the other distribution warnings should be treated as risk flags, not short recommendations.
- Because broker-direct coverage is zero, estimated smart-money cost, broker persistence/concentration, top accumulator identity, and direct reversal evidence are absent. Those are the key variables expected to materially reshape the ranking once the dedicated Supabase project and broker ingestion are active.
